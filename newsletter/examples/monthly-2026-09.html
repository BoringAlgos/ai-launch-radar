Idea of the month

ReconPilot — GST reconciliation copilot for Indian SMBs

The problem

GSTR-2B reconciliation — matching the purchase register against the auto-generated credit statement — is "the single most time-consuming exercise" for many firms. The India SME Forum estimates the Invoice Management System adds ~Rs.1.5 lakh/year in compliance cost for MSMEs; unreconciled mismatches routinely cost small businesses Rs.10,000-50,000 a year in lost input tax credit.

The idea

A copilot for the monthly reconciliation run: ingest the purchase register (Tally/Zoho CSV), pull GSTR-2B, match every invoice. Jev makes the judgment calls rules can't — do these two invoices match despite the GSTIN typo? (noul), mismatch severity (score), IMS action (choice: accept / reject / pending). Drafts vendor chase messages and a one-page mismatch report for the CA. Start CSV-in/CSV-out (weekend MVP); graduate to a Jev-driven browser agent for the portal.
