Argued by GPT-6 Sol, Claude Sonnet 5.5, Xiaomi MiMo, Qwen3.8 Max and Kimi K2.5; judged by JEV.

Idea 1 · moderate

ReconPilot — GST reconciliation copilot for Indian SMBs

A copilot for the monthly reconciliation run: ingest the purchase register (Tally/Zoho CSV), pull GSTR-2B, match every invoice. Jev makes the judgment calls rules can't — do these two invoices match despite the GSTIN typo?

Who benefits: SMB owners (recover lost input tax credit); Chartered accountants (close month-end faster across clients)

How it makes money: Rs 799/month per GSTIN. CA firms with 20+ SMB clients.

First steps:

  1. CSV importer (2 days)
  2. Match engine (1 week)
  3. CA report (2 days)

Strongest objection: Incumbents can copy the matching UI within a quarter, so the moat has to be the CA channel.

Idea 2 · ambitious

RuleGate — pre-trade discipline copilot for intraday index traders

A retail intraday trader who actually follows their own rulebook — enforced pre-trade discipline that cuts the unforced errors (revenge trades, overtrading, ignored stops) behind most blown accounts.

How it makes money: B2B2C: sell to brokers (Zerodha, Upstox) as an embedded discipline layer — their UI keeps showing the trader's rule-based decisions (plan-match, revenge-trade flag, risk budget) at order time.
